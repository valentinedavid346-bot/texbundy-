Ted Bundy
November 24, 1946  —  January 24, 1989
By David Romero, Kahari Bradley & Jhostin Bonifacio
A Case That Changed Forensic Science Forever
He confessed to 30 murders across 7 states.
Investigators believe the real number exceeds 100.
He escaped from custody twice.
His trials pioneered bite mark evidence in American courts.
"He looked like the boy next door.
That was the most terrifying part."
